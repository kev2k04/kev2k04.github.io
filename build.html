Passion Project: The Build Behind This Website

I have always been someone who was super into technology. When I was younger, that meant comparing the newest tablet specs on the market so that I could play games with better performance, learning about monitor refresh rates and the advantages they gave the users, or looking for software that could help me optimize the tedious tasks I was working on. I have always enjoyed exploring cool solutions to boring problems, and this is just a small example of that curiosity within me!

I remember that a couple of years ago, I tried coding a basic website with HTML and CSS just to show my engineering friends that I could write some code. Even though I had never actually learned how to code properly, I made a website that you could navigate and "use" (even though it looked like absolute garbage). Looking back now, it is incredible to see what learning to utilize Claude Code has helped me build through prompt engineering alone, and I'm so glad to have done the learning behind such a tool.

I started this website as a passion project because I wanted to understand how to work alongside the newest edition of AI tools like Claude Code. It has been a great introduction to what has come out in recent years, with generative AI like ChatGPT only being released to the public in 2022. I see this as just the beginning of a much longer journey, and with so much ambiguity around what the future will look like, I want to stay ahead of the curve by experimenting with new software, experimenting with them, and finding creative ways to use them that others may not have thought of yet.

Instead of making this website feel like a typical finance portfolio that only lists professional experiences and LinkedIn-style achievements, I wanted it to feel more personal. I wanted this site to be a place where people can actually learn what kind of person I am, and hopefully relate to my passions, my hobbies, the way I think, and the things that make me curious.
